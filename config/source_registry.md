## Portal-to-official procedure
1. Search exact job ID on employer-controlled systems.
2. Search exact title + company + location.
3. Search the employer ATS.
4. Search the official careers site.
5. Promote only when a specific official live page is found.
6. Otherwise retain the lead as portal-only or manual verification.

## Evidence standard
- A homepage or generic search page does not prove a company was checked.
- A checked company requires official job interface or ATS inspection and a recorded result, no-match, or access outcome.
